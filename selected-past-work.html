Swiggy Instamart · Checkout & fulfilment · 2020

Deliver Later.

02 / 02

Not every grocery order needs to arrive immediately. Deliver Later introduced scheduled delivery as an alternative to instant delivery, giving shoppers the choice to plan around their day while supporting more efficient fulfilment.

My contribution

Leverage the existing framework to design the pre-order and post-order journeys for slotted delivery.

Design challenge

Introduce a new delivery model without losing the clarity of the existing instant-delivery checkout.

Business objectives

Designing for adoption and delivery economics.

The launch brief defined two success targets. These are objectives, not achieved results.

Adoption target
~40%Consumer adoption of slotted delivery

Within six weeks of launch.

Cost target
₹37Total cost per slotted delivery

Target for the scheduled-delivery model.

Final approach

One checkout. Two delivery promises.

Bring the choice into checkout, make the cost and timing explicit, and allow the customer to confirm their preferred window before making payment.

Original checkout explorations. Tap any screen to enlarge. The fees shown are from the historical design, not current Instamart pricing.

The design principle

Make the trade-off legible.

In the final approach, instant delivery was shown at ₹35 and scheduled delivery at ₹25. The ₹10 saving was visible alongside the timing, so customers could compare convenience and cost before choosing.
