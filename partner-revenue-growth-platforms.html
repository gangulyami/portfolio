Instamart / Advertising & monetisation

Brand Portal

01 / 05

I led the design evolution from a keyword-ads tool to a self-serve Brand Portal. I shaped campaign workflows hands-on, validated them with advertisers and coached the designers who led individual launches. The portal serves small businesses, D2C brands, large FMCG companies and agencies.

Launch

We simplified campaign creation into a reusable setup flow and opened up new self-serve placements, including pre-search banner bidding. Brands can also run sampling campaigns directly.

Optimise

Suggested bids, keyword share of voice, automatic renewals and daily budget rollover give advertisers more control over how campaigns run.

Measure

Sales reporting and Brand Insights show partners how campaigns are performing across sales, spend and return on ad spend.
